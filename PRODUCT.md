# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Harvestly is for Cambodian rice and cassava farmers in rural provinces who use basic Android phones, may have little or no digital literacy, and need help while standing in a field with crops turning yellow or showing disease symptoms. Agricultural officers may not be nearby or immediately reachable.

## Product Purpose

The primary job is simple and urgent: take or select a crop photo, receive an understandable diagnosis, and immediately see practical next actions. The product succeeds when a farmer can recognize risk, act on treatment guidance, and feel supported without needing technical vocabulary or a lengthy workflow.

## Brand Personality

**Grounded. Urgent. Trustworthy.**

The product should feel local, legible, serious, and useful under pressure. It should respect the farmer's context and present technology as a dependable field tool, not a distant expert system.

## Anti-references

- Grab or AirAsia app styling: too corporate and city-focused.
- Duolingo-like gamification: too playful for crop loss and livelihood risk.
- Silicon Valley agri-tech startup UI: too white, clean, and foreign.
- ChatGPT or Claude-style interfaces: too text-heavy and sterile for field use.

## Design Principles

1. **Put diagnosis first.** The camera or gallery action is the entry point; supporting guidance and history follow the diagnosis task.
2. **Speak locally.** Khmer is the default farmer-facing language; English is an explicit opt-in for secondary users or support staff.
3. **Earn trust through clarity.** Diagnosis, confidence, risk, and treatment must be visible and understandable without interpretation.
4. **Design for the field.** High contrast, large tap targets, low cognitive load, and outdoor readability come before ornamental detail.
5. **Use Cambodian identity with restraint.** Heritage cues should reinforce trust and place without slowing the crop-scanning workflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Prioritize readability in bright sunlight with strong contrast and clear hierarchy.
- Use large, one-hand touch targets suitable for basic Android phones in the field.
- Keep Khmer text readable at practical sizes with simple, direct wording.
- Provide unmistakable selected, uploading, analyzing, result, disabled, and error states.
- Reduce dependence on reading-heavy instructions; keep actions obvious and brief.
- Treat weak connectivity and audio guidance as valuable future needs, after the required outdoor and touch usability baseline.

## Initial Product Boundaries

- Rice and cassava diagnosis share the first product experience; crop-specific expansion can follow later.
- English remains available as a secondary mode; Khmer remains the default product language.
- This document defines product and design direction, not diagnosis accuracy or backend model capability.
